How It Works

A simple rhythm: submit, review, pray, and encourage.

1. Submit

Share your request privately. It enters a review queue before being posted publicly.

2. Pray

Visitors read through approved requests and click “Pray For” after taking time to pray.

3. Encourage

The requester receives a gentle email letting them know someone prayed for them.

The Prayer Project exists to encourage, comfort, and remind people that they are not forgotten.
