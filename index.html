How It Works

The goal is simple: make it easier for people to ask for prayer and easier for others to respond with compassion.

1. Submit

Someone shares a prayer request with a title, category, message, and private email for notifications.

2. Pray

Visitors scroll through the wall and click “Pray For” when they pause to pray for a request.

3. Encourage

The requester receives a warm email letting them know they were remembered in prayer.

The Prayer Project exists to encourage, comfort, and remind people that they are not forgotten.
