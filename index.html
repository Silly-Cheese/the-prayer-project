How It Works

A simple rhythm: submit, pray, encourage, and protect the space when needed.

1. Submit

Share your request privately. It posts publicly without your name attached.

2. Pray

Visitors read through requests and click “Pray For” after taking time to pray.

3. Protect

If a request is reported, it is hidden and sent to the admin dashboard for review.
