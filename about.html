About the Mission

A quiet place for prayer, comfort, and hope.

The Prayer Project was created for the moments when someone needs prayer but does not know how to ask, who to ask, or how much to explain. It is meant to be simple, reverent, and personal.

1

Share without pressure

People can submit a request without attaching their name or turning their burden into a public performance.

2

Pray with intention

Visitors are encouraged to slow down, read carefully, and click only after actually taking time to pray.

3

Encourage quietly

The requester receives a gentle email reminder that someone saw their request and prayed for them.

Why It Exists

People should not feel forgotten.

A small reminder can matter deeply when someone is carrying grief, anxiety, uncertainty, or fear.

There are moments when people do not know who to talk to, how to explain what they feel, or whether anyone would care. The Prayer Project is meant to become a small but meaningful reminder that burdens do not have to be carried alone.

The goal is not attention. The goal is prayer. Every request is treated with dignity, and every prayer click is meant to be a quiet act of care.

Requests post publicly right away so people do not have to wait for encouragement. If a request is reported, it is hidden until reviewed by an administrator so the space can remain respectful and safe.

“Bear one another's burdens, and so fulfill the law of Christ.”Galatians 6:2

Every request matters

There are no featured prayers, rankings, popularity sections, or promoted requests. Each burden deserves care, whether one person has prayed or many have.

Prayer and safety together

Prayer matters, and practical help matters too. Crisis resources are included because spiritual encouragement should never prevent someone from getting urgent support.

A simple place to begin.

Submit a request, pray for someone else, or read through the wall quietly. Even a small act of prayer can become a meaningful encouragement.
