About the Mission

A quiet place for prayer, comfort, and hope.

The Prayer Project was created to give people a peaceful way to ask for prayer without needing to explain everything publicly, and to give others a simple way to respond with compassion.

Anonymous by Design

People can share what they are comfortable sharing without placing their name on the request.

Built for Encouragement

When someone clicks “Pray For,” the requester receives an email reminding them that someone prayed.

Simple and Reverent

The site is designed to feel peaceful instead of busy, loud, or social-media driven.

Why this matters

There are moments when people do not know who to talk to, how to explain what they feel, or whether anyone would care. This project is meant to become a small but meaningful reminder that burdens do not have to be carried alone.

The goal is not attention. The goal is prayer. Every request is treated with dignity, and every prayer click is meant to be a quiet act of care.

“Bear one another's burdens, and so fulfill the law of Christ.”Galatians 6:2
The Prayer Project exists to encourage, comfort, and remind people that they are not forgotten.
