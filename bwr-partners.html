Why Partnerships Matter

The people closest to a need often understand it best.

A good initiative should not arrive in a community assuming it already knows everything.

Partnership is how BWR listens before it acts.

A church may know families quietly struggling. A shelter may understand what people experiencing homelessness actually need. A community organization may already have trusted relationships we could never build overnight. A supplier may know how to make each dollar stretch farther. A business may be able to solve a practical problem we have not even encountered yet.

That is why partnership is not an afterthought for Bibles Within Reach. It is part of the plan from the beginning.

We are looking for useful, honest relationships where each side understands its role, recipients are treated with dignity, and the work becomes stronger because it is being done together.

“For we are labourers together with God.”1 Corinthians 3:9
Ways to Partner

You do not have to do everything to make a difference.

Choose the role or roles that actually fit your organization. The application lets you select more than one.

01

Distribution Partners

Churches, ministries, shelters, outreach organizations, and other trusted groups may help place Bibles with people they already know and serve.

Potential role: receive approved quantities, distribute voluntarily and respectfully, and report what was actually distributed.
02

Community & Referral Partners

Some organizations may help BWR understand local needs, connect with the right people, or identify communities where access to Scripture is limited.

Potential role: local insight, introductions, referrals, outreach coordination, and community knowledge.
03

Business & Supplier Partners

Businesses, retailers, publishers, vendors, and logistics partners may be able to support sourcing, pricing, transport, packaging, storage, or other practical needs.

Potential role: supplier support, in-kind help, preferred pricing, transportation, logistics, or other mission-aligned assistance.
04

Outreach & Event Partners

Some partners may serve alongside The Prayer Project during direct outreach or help create appropriate opportunities where Bibles can be offered.

Potential role: outreach locations, volunteers, local coordination, event support, and respectful distribution environments.

Partnership Application

Tell us where your organization fits.

This application is designed to begin a real review—not force every organization into the same mold. Give us enough information to understand who you are, who you serve, and what kind of relationship may make sense.

  • There is no application fee.
  • Applying does not guarantee approval or Bible quantities.
  • Recipients should never be pressured to accept Scripture.
  • Distribution partners will be expected to report Bibles distributed.
  • Do not include private information about individual recipients.
