Crisis Support Resources

The Prayer Project exists to encourage people spiritually, but it is not a replacement for emergency or crisis support services.

Emergency Situations

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services immediately.

Mental Health Crisis

In the United States and Canada, you can call or text 988 to reach the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline.

You Are Not Forgotten

Even when things feel overwhelming, reaching out for help is important. Prayer and professional support can exist together.
