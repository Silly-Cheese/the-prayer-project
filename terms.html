Terms of Service

Use this space with care.

By using The Prayer Project, you agree to use the platform respectfully, honestly, and responsibly.

Purpose

The Prayer Project exists to provide a calm place for prayer requests and encouragement. It is not a social media platform, emergency service, counseling service, or substitute for professional support.

Prohibited content

  • Harassment, threats, bullying, or abusive content
  • Spam, jokes, fake requests, or intentionally misleading submissions
  • Sexually explicit content
  • Private identifying information about another person without consent
  • Instructions or encouragement for harm, violence, abuse, or illegal activity

Reports and moderation

Requests are posted immediately, but reported requests are hidden until reviewed by an administrator. Administrators may approve, remove, or delete content as needed to protect the platform.

No emergency services

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, contact emergency services. The Prayer Project cannot provide emergency response.

Prayer and professional support can exist together. Reaching out for help is not a lack of faith.

Availability

Features, email notifications, moderation systems, and content access may change or become unavailable without notice.

The Prayer Project exists to encourage, comfort, and remind people that they are not forgotten.
