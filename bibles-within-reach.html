The Purpose

Scripture should not feel out of reach.

The Prayer Project believes that a person's financial circumstances should not be the reason they cannot have a Bible of their own. Bibles Within Reach is designed to turn generosity into something tangible: Scripture placed directly into the hands of people who want it.

1

Raise support

Run a focused six-month public campaign that gives people a clear way to support Bible purchasing and responsible distribution.

2

Purchase Bibles

Use campaign resources to purchase Bibles in appropriate formats and quantities once supplier pricing, demand, and distribution capacity are known.

3

Give them freely

Provide those Bibles without charge through direct outreach and trusted community relationships, with dignity and respect for every recipient.

Why It Exists

A Bible of your own should not be a luxury.

People experiencing homelessness, financial hardship, displacement, reentry, or other difficult circumstances may not have easy access to a Bible they can keep and use.

Bibles Within Reach is not intended to be a short collection drive. It is being developed as an organized Prayer Project outreach initiative with a defined campaign period, intentional purchasing, responsible distribution, and public impact reporting.

The initiative begins with a simple conviction: if someone wants access to Scripture, The Prayer Project should look for practical ways to help place it within reach. That means more than collecting money. It means preparing distribution relationships, selecting appropriate Bibles, respecting recipients, tracking the work, and learning how the effort can continue.

“So shall my word be that goeth forth out of my mouth: it shall not return unto me void.”Isaiah 55:11
How Distribution Will Work

From campaign support to a Bible in someone's hands.

The final distribution model will be refined before launch, but the initiative is being built around direct outreach and trusted local relationships rather than simply purchasing Bibles without a plan for where they will go.

Step 1

Identify needs

TPP will seek appropriate distribution opportunities through volunteers, chapters, churches, shelters, ministries, and community relationships where practical.

Step 2

Match the supply

Bible quantities and formats can be selected with real distribution needs in mind instead of ordering blindly.

Step 3

Distribute with dignity

Bibles will be offered freely and respectfully. Receiving Scripture will not require a purchase, donation, membership, or participation in another TPP program.

Step 4

Record the impact

TPP plans to track purchasing and distribution totals so supporters can see what the campaign actually accomplished.

Who Can Receive a Bible?

People who need or request one.

The initial focus is on people experiencing homelessness or financial hardship, while leaving room to serve others who genuinely lack reliable access to Scripture. The goal is access, not creating unnecessary barriers or forcing someone to prove their hardship.

Distribution Principles
FreeNo Bible purchased through the initiative is intended for resale to a recipient.
RespectfulRecipients should be treated with dignity, not as statistics or promotional material.
VoluntaryNo one should be pressured to accept a Bible or required to participate in another activity to receive one.
ResponsibleDistribution should be organized in ways that make good use of the resources entrusted to the initiative.

Future Program

Request a Bible.

As the initiative matures, TPP can build a public request process for individuals and a separate request process for approved organizations seeking Bibles for distribution.

The future request system is intended to make the initiative more responsive instead of relying only on TPP to guess where Bibles are needed. The request process is not active yet. Before it opens, TPP will need to establish availability, fulfillment rules, privacy expectations, distribution capacity, and how requests are prioritized when demand exceeds supply.

Frequently Asked Questions

What to know before the campaign begins.

When does Bibles Within Reach launch?

The current plan is to publicly launch the initiative in late May 2027 and run the fundraising campaign for approximately six months, through late November 2027.

Can I donate right now?

No. This page is currently a pre-launch information page. The Prayer Project will publish the official donation method when the campaign opens.

Who will receive the Bibles?

The initial focus is on people experiencing homelessness, financial hardship, or other barriers to obtaining Scripture, while leaving room to serve others who genuinely need or request a Bible.

Will recipients have to pay?

No. Bibles purchased through Bibles Within Reach are intended to be distributed free of charge to recipients.

How will Bibles be distributed?

TPP plans to use organized direct outreach and appropriate relationships with volunteers, chapters, churches, shelters, ministries, and community organizations where practical. The exact distribution network will continue developing before launch.

What will campaign support pay for?

The central purpose is Bible purchasing. Reasonable initiative-related costs may also include needs such as shipping, packaging, storage, transportation, or other distribution expenses necessary to carry out the program.

Will TPP publish campaign results?

Yes. The initiative page is designed to later display meaningful information such as funds raised, Bibles purchased, Bibles distributed, communities served, and other campaign milestones.

Can a church or organization request Bibles?

Not yet. A partner or organization request process is a planned future capability once TPP establishes fulfillment procedures, availability rules, and distribution capacity.

Scripture & Ministry

Why Bible distribution belongs within The Prayer Project.

The Prayer Project began with prayer: helping people bring burdens before God and reminding them they are not forgotten. Bibles Within Reach carries that same mission into tangible outreach by helping people gain access to the Scriptures that teach, encourage, correct, comfort, and point to Christ.

This initiative is not meant to replace prayer, local churches, community ministries, or practical assistance. It is one way The Prayer Project can serve alongside them—by making Scripture more accessible and giving people something they can continue reading long after a single outreach interaction ends.
