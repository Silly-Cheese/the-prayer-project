The Moment Behind the Mission

What if the Bible is always the thing that has to wait?

When every dollar has somewhere more urgent to go, even something deeply wanted can keep getting pushed to next week, next month, or someday.

Bibles Within Reach exists for that “someday.”

Think about someone choosing between groceries and a Bible, bus fare and a Bible, medicine and a Bible, or simply keeping the few dollars they have in their pocket for whatever tomorrow brings. In that situation, not buying a Bible does not mean Scripture matters less. It may simply mean survival matters first.

The Prayer Project cannot remove every hardship in a person's life. But we can look at one barrier and say: this one does not have to stay here.

That is the purpose of Bibles Within Reach. Not to create a photo opportunity. Not to count people as numbers. Not to make anyone sit through something before receiving help. The goal is much quieter than that: when someone wants a Bible, we want to be able to place one in their hands and say, “This is yours.”

“Thy word is a lamp unto my feet, and a light unto my path.”Psalm 119:105
The Mission

Simple enough to explain in one sentence.

Raise support, purchase Bibles carefully, and give them to people who want Scripture but face a real financial or practical barrier to getting a Bible of their own.

01

Wanted, not forced.

Bibles Within Reach is for people who want Scripture. Distribution should feel like an invitation, never a condition, a sales pitch, or pressure.

Receiving a Bible should always be voluntary.
02

Free means free.

A Bible purchased through this initiative is intended to be placed with a recipient at no cost. No fee. No repayment. No catch.

The point is to remove a barrier, not replace it with another one.
03

Dignity before numbers.

A successful campaign is not just a large total. It is careful stewardship, meaningful distribution, and people being treated like people at every step.

Impact matters more than optics.

Who We Hope to Reach

People first. Always.

Tulsa will be the first service area. The categories below help define where Bibles Within Reach can begin, but nobody should ever feel reduced to a category in order to receive Scripture.

Direct Outreach

People experiencing homelessness

We plan to create opportunities to offer Bibles directly to people experiencing homelessness who want Scripture they can keep.

Financial Barriers

Individuals and families under pressure

When money is tight, a Bible can become a purchase that keeps getting postponed. This initiative exists to help remove that barrier.

Local Relationships

People served by trusted organizations

Churches and appropriate community organizations may help identify genuine needs and place Bibles with people they already know and serve.

Access

Anyone facing a real barrier to obtaining one

The mission is not limited to one circumstance. When someone genuinely wants a Bible and access is difficult, Bibles Within Reach should be able to respond responsibly.

No pressure. No required photo. No performance. No one should be pressured to receive a Bible, photographed as a condition of receiving one, or required to participate in another activity first. The point is access to Scripture with dignity and respect.

Frequently Asked Questions

The things worth knowing before launch.

What is Bibles Within Reach?

Bibles Within Reach is a Prayer Project initiative being developed to raise support, purchase Bibles, and provide them free of charge to people who want Scripture but may face financial or practical barriers to obtaining a Bible of their own.

When does the campaign begin?

The current plan is to publicly launch in late May 2027 and run the first fundraising campaign for approximately six months, through late November 2027.

What is the campaign goal?

The planned first-campaign goal is $50,000. Final purchasing, supplier, financial, and distribution details will be published before public fundraising opens.

Can I donate right now?

No. Bibles Within Reach is still in pre-launch development and this page is not accepting contributions yet. The approved support method will be published when the campaign officially opens.

Who will receive the Bibles?

The first campaign will focus especially on people experiencing homelessness, individuals and families facing financial hardship, and others who genuinely want a Bible but do not have an easy way to obtain one.

Where will the first Bibles go?

Tulsa, Oklahoma is the first planned service area. Distribution may include direct Prayer Project outreach and appropriate placement through trusted local churches or community organizations.

Will anyone have to pay or participate in something first?

No. Bibles purchased through the initiative are intended to be given free of charge, voluntarily, and respectfully. A recipient should not be required to pay, pose for a photo, attend an event, or participate in another activity as a condition of receiving one.

What will campaign support be used for?

Bible purchasing is the central purpose. Reasonable initiative-related needs may also include shipping, packaging, storage, transportation, or other costs necessary to move Bibles from a supplier into responsible distribution. Final campaign details will be disclosed before launch.

How will I know what the campaign accomplished?

This page is designed to become a public impact dashboard after launch. Meaningful totals such as funds raised, Bibles purchased, Bibles distributed, locations reached, and major milestones will be added as the work progresses.
