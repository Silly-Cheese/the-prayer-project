Why This Exists

There are people who want a Bible and still do not have one.

Sometimes the reason is money. Sometimes it is access. Sometimes life has simply made one more purchase impossible to justify.

We cannot remove every obstacle someone is facing. We can remove this one.

Bibles Within Reach started with a simple thought: if someone genuinely wants Scripture, the price of a Bible should not be what stands in the way.

That matters for people experiencing homelessness, families under financial pressure, young people without easy access to a Bible of their own, and others who may be surrounded by needs that feel more urgent than buying a book—even when that book matters deeply to them.

This is not about chasing the biggest distribution number we can post online. It is about serving people carefully. It is about a Bible offered without pressure, handed to someone respectfully, and placed where it can actually be read, carried, kept, and returned to.

Partnerships Begin Before Fundraising

We do not want to build this alone.

The strongest version of Bibles Within Reach will be shaped by people who already know their communities. We want to begin conversations now with churches, shelters, ministries, outreach organizations, businesses, and others who may be able to help us find needs, open doors, supply Bibles, support logistics, or distribute Scripture responsibly.

A partnership does not mean doing everything. Some partners may help us understand where Bibles are needed. Some may help place them. Some may help us purchase or move them. The right role depends on the organization and the people it serves.

The 2027 Campaign

A big goal with a very human purpose.

The planned first Bibles Within Reach campaign will aim to raise $50,000. That number is intentionally ambitious because the vision is bigger than a one-day Bible drive: it is to build a repeatable, accountable way to purchase and distribute Scripture at meaningful scale.

We will not pretend today that every dollar equals a fixed number of Bibles. Supplier pricing, quantities, shipping, storage, transportation, and real distribution capacity all matter. Final campaign details will be published before fundraising opens.

What Will Not Change

The way we serve matters as much as what we give.

Bibles Within Reach should feel personal on the receiving end and trustworthy on the supporting end. These are the standards we want the initiative to keep from the first conversation through every distribution.

Dignity

People are not campaign content.

No one should be treated like a photo opportunity, a number, or a condition for proving impact.

Voluntary

A Bible is offered, never forced.

Receiving Scripture should always be a person's choice. No pressure and no required activity attached.

Stewardship

Resources should make sense.

Purchasing and distribution decisions should be practical, documented, and focused on the mission.

Transparency

Supporters should see the work.

When the campaign begins, real numbers should replace promises: funds, Bibles, distribution, and meaningful milestones.
