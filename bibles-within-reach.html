Bibles Within Reach.

Putting the Word of God within reach. Bibles Within Reach is a six-month Prayer Project fundraising and outreach initiative dedicated to purchasing Bibles and providing them free of charge to people who may otherwise have difficulty obtaining one.

The Purpose

Scripture should not feel out of reach.

The Prayer Project believes access to a Bible should not depend on someone's financial circumstances. This initiative is designed to turn generosity into something tangible: Bibles placed directly into the hands of people who need them.

1

Raise support

For approximately six months, The Prayer Project will raise designated support for the initiative and prepare for responsible purchasing and distribution.

2

Purchase Bibles

After the campaign period, raised funds will be used to purchase Bibles for free distribution as part of The Prayer Project's outreach ministry.

3

Give them freely

Bibles purchased through the initiative will be distributed without charge to people experiencing homelessness, financial hardship, or other barriers to obtaining Scripture.

Who We Hope to Serve

People who may not have easy access to a Bible.

The initial focus is on individuals experiencing homelessness or financial hardship, while leaving room to serve others who genuinely lack reliable access to Scripture.

Bibles Within Reach is not intended to be a short collection drive. It is being developed as an organized Prayer Project outreach initiative with a defined fundraising period, planned purchasing, and intentional distribution.

The goal is simple: receive support responsibly, turn that support into Bibles, and then give those Bibles away. As the initiative develops, The Prayer Project plans to share campaign updates and later report tangible impact such as Bibles purchased and distributed.
