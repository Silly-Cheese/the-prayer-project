Why This Matters

Imagine wanting a Bible—and deciding it can wait.

For many people, the next purchase has to be food, transportation, rent, medicine, or something their family needs today.

Bibles Within Reach exists for the Bible that keeps getting pushed to “later.”

The idea behind Bibles Within Reach is simple. If someone wants Scripture, money should not be the thing standing between that person and a Bible they can keep.

That is especially meaningful for people experiencing homelessness, families under financial pressure, and others who may not have convenient access to a church, bookstore, online order, or spare money for a Bible. The Prayer Project cannot solve every hardship a person faces, but this is one practical need we can choose to meet.

So the goal is not to hand out as many books as possible for a photograph or a number. It is to serve real people with dignity—to offer a Bible freely, without pressure, and to build a program careful enough to keep doing that well.

“Thy word is a lamp unto my feet, and a light unto my path.”Psalm 119:105
The Mission

Not just a Bible drive. A way to make access practical.

Bibles Within Reach is designed as a focused Prayer Project initiative with a defined campaign, intentional purchasing, organized distribution, and public reporting. The work is meant to be simple enough to understand and careful enough to trust.

01

Raise support with a purpose.

The public campaign will have one clear mission: help make Bibles available to people who may otherwise have difficulty obtaining one.

Planned campaign window: late May–late November 2027.
02

Give every Bible freely.

Who We Hope to Reach

People first. Always.

The first campaign will begin in Tulsa and focus on people for whom access to a Bible may genuinely be difficult. The categories below help define the mission, but they do not reduce anyone to a label.

Direct Outreach

People experiencing homelessness

The Prayer Project plans to create opportunities in Tulsa to offer Bibles directly to people experiencing homelessness who want Scripture of their own.

Families & Individuals

People facing financial hardship

When money is tight, a Bible can easily become a purchase that has to wait. This initiative exists to help remove that barrier.

Local Relationships

People already served by trusted organizations

Churches and appropriate community organizations may help identify genuine needs and place Bibles with people and families they already know and serve.

Access

Others who simply do not have an easy way to get one

The mission is not limited to one circumstance. When there is a real barrier and a real desire for Scripture, Bibles Within Reach should be able to respond responsibly.

Always voluntary. No one should be pressured to receive a Bible, photographed as a condition of receiving one, or required to participate in another activity. The goal is access to Scripture with dignity and respect.

Frequently Asked Questions

A few things worth knowing.

What is Bibles Within Reach?

Bibles Within Reach is a Prayer Project initiative being developed to raise support, purchase Bibles, and provide them free of charge to people who want Scripture but may face financial or practical barriers to obtaining a Bible of their own.

When does the campaign begin?

The current plan is to publicly launch in late May 2027 and run the fundraising campaign for approximately six months, through late November 2027.

What is the campaign goal?

The planned first-campaign goal is $50,000. Final purchasing, supplier, financial, and distribution details will be published before the public campaign opens.

Can I contribute right now?

No. This page is currently a pre-launch information page and is not accepting contributions for Bibles Within Reach yet. The approved support method will be published when the campaign officially opens.

Where will the first Bibles go?

Tulsa, Oklahoma is the initiative's first service area. The plan includes direct in-person outreach and appropriate placement with trusted local churches or community organizations that already serve people and families who may need a Bible.

Who will receive the Bibles?

The first campaign will focus especially on people experiencing homelessness, families or individuals facing financial hardship, and others who genuinely want a Bible but do not have easy access to one.

Will anyone have to pay for a Bible?

No. Bibles purchased through Bibles Within Reach are intended to be distributed free of charge to recipients.

Will anyone be pressured to accept a Bible?

No. Distribution should be voluntary and respectful. No person should be pressured to receive a Bible or required to participate in another activity as a condition of receiving one.

What will campaign support be used for?

Bible purchasing is the central purpose of the campaign. Reasonable initiative-related needs may also include shipping, packaging, storage, transportation, or other costs necessary to move Bibles from a supplier into responsible distribution. Final campaign details will be disclosed before launch.

Will The Prayer Project show what the campaign accomplished?

Yes. This page is designed to become an impact dashboard once the campaign begins, with meaningful information such as funds raised, Bibles purchased, Bibles distributed, locations reached, and major initiative milestones.
