The Purpose

Scripture should not feel out of reach.

The Prayer Project believes that a person's financial circumstances should not be the reason they cannot have a Bible of their own. Bibles Within Reach is designed to turn generosity into something tangible: Scripture placed directly into the hands of people who want it.

1

Raise support

Run a focused six-month public campaign that gives people a clear way to support Bible purchasing and responsible distribution.

2

Purchase Bibles

Use campaign resources to purchase Bibles in appropriate formats and quantities once supplier pricing, demand, and distribution capacity are known.

3

Give them freely

Provide those Bibles without charge through direct outreach in Tulsa and trusted local distribution relationships, with dignity and respect for every recipient.

Why It Exists

A Bible of your own should not be a luxury.

People experiencing homelessness, families facing financial hardship, and others in difficult circumstances may not have easy access to a Bible they can keep and use.

Bibles Within Reach is not intended to be a short collection drive. It is being developed as an organized Prayer Project outreach initiative with a defined campaign period, intentional purchasing, responsible distribution, and public impact reporting.

The initiative begins with a simple conviction: if someone wants access to Scripture, The Prayer Project should look for practical ways to help place it within reach. The initial plan is intentionally local and hands-on: distribute Bibles directly in Tulsa while also placing Bibles with trusted local organizations that already know and serve families in their communities.

“So shall my word be that goeth forth out of my mouth: it shall not return unto me void.”Isaiah 55:11
How Distribution Will Work

A clear plan for where the Bibles will go.

Bibles Within Reach is being built around direct, in-person outreach in Tulsa and carefully coordinated local distribution relationships. The goal is for supporters to be able to clearly understand who the initiative is trying to serve and how purchased Bibles will reach them.

Step 1

Start in Tulsa

Tulsa, Oklahoma will be the initiative's initial service area. TPP plans to identify places and circumstances where people are experiencing homelessness, financial hardship, or limited access to Scripture.

Step 2

Hand Bibles out directly

A primary part of the plan is physical, in-person distribution. TPP intends to place Bibles directly into the hands of people who want one rather than relying only on indirect distribution.

Step 3

Equip local organizations

Some Bibles may be provided to local churches and appropriately participating schools, school-affiliated programs, or community organizations that can help reach families and individuals they already serve.

Step 4

Track where they go

TPP plans to record purchasing and distribution totals, including direct distributions and Bibles placed with local organizations, so supporters can see what the campaign accomplished.

About school distribution: Any distribution involving a school will be coordinated only where participation is appropriate and permitted. The purpose is to make Bibles available to people who want them, not to pressure anyone to receive one.

Who We Intend to Serve

A defined initial audience.

The first campaign is intended to focus resources where access to a Bible may genuinely be limited, with Tulsa serving as the starting point.

People experiencing homelessness

Direct Tulsa outreach will prioritize opportunities to offer Bibles to people experiencing homelessness who want Scripture of their own.

Families facing financial hardship

Families who may not be able to comfortably purchase a Bible will be an important part of the initiative's intended reach, including through local churches and other appropriate community relationships.

Others without easy access

The initiative can also serve people who genuinely need or request a Bible but do not have a practical way to obtain one. The goal is access without unnecessary barriers.

Two Distribution Paths

Direct outreach + local placement.

Some Bibles will be handed out directly through TPP outreach in Tulsa. Others may be placed with churches and appropriate local schools, school-affiliated programs, or community organizations so they can reach people and families they already know.

Distribution Principles
FreeNo recipient will be charged for a Bible purchased through the initiative.

Frequently Asked Questions

What to know before the campaign begins.

When does Bibles Within Reach launch?

The current plan is to publicly launch the initiative in late May 2027 and run the fundraising campaign for approximately six months, through late November 2027.

Can I donate right now?

No. This page is currently a pre-launch information page. The Prayer Project will publish the official donation method when the campaign opens.

Where will the first Bibles be distributed?

Tulsa, Oklahoma is the initial focus. TPP plans to physically distribute Bibles through direct outreach there and may also provide Bibles to trusted local churches and appropriately participating schools, school-affiliated programs, or community organizations for distribution to people and families they serve.

Who is the target audience?

The first campaign will focus especially on people experiencing homelessness, families facing financial hardship, and others who genuinely want or need a Bible but do not have easy access to one.

Will recipients have to pay?

No. Bibles purchased through Bibles Within Reach are intended to be distributed free of charge to recipients.

How will Bibles be distributed?

The plan has two main paths: direct, in-person TPP distribution in Tulsa and local placement with trusted churches and other appropriate organizations that can help reach people and families they already serve.

What will campaign support pay for?

The central purpose is Bible purchasing. Reasonable initiative-related costs may also include needs such as shipping, packaging, storage, transportation, or other distribution expenses necessary to carry out the program.

Will TPP publish campaign results?

Yes. The initiative page is designed to later display meaningful information such as funds raised, Bibles purchased, Bibles distributed, Tulsa-area locations reached, and other campaign milestones.

Can a church, school program, or organization request Bibles?

Not yet. A partner or organization request process is a planned future capability once TPP establishes fulfillment procedures, availability rules, and distribution capacity.

Scripture & Ministry

Why Bible distribution belongs within The Prayer Project.

The Prayer Project began with prayer: helping people bring burdens before God and reminding them they are not forgotten. Bibles Within Reach carries that same mission into tangible outreach by helping people gain access to the Scriptures that teach, encourage, correct, comfort, and point to Christ.

This initiative is not meant to replace prayer, local churches, community ministries, schools, or practical assistance. It is one way The Prayer Project can serve alongside the community—by making Scripture more accessible and giving people something they can continue reading long after a single outreach interaction ends.
