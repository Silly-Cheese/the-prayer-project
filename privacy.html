Privacy Policy

The Prayer Project values privacy, dignity, and respect for every prayer request submitted through the platform.

Information Collected

  • Prayer request content
  • Email addresses for prayer notifications
  • Prayer interaction statistics
  • Reports submitted by users

Email Privacy

Email addresses are stored separately from public prayer requests and are not displayed publicly on the prayer wall.

Moderation

Prayer requests may be reviewed by moderators before appearing publicly in order to reduce spam, harassment, or harmful content.

Safety Notice

The Prayer Project is not a crisis intervention or emergency support service.

If you are in immediate danger or experiencing a mental health emergency, contact local emergency services or a crisis hotline.
