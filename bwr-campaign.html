Enough room to think beyond a one-day Bible drive.

Bible purchasing is the heart of the campaign. The current plan is to purchase primarily through Mardel, with SKU 3660396 identified as the preferred Bible option. Final pricing, quantities, availability, shipping, and any bulk terms will be finalized before fundraising opens.

Partner Distribution Accountability

A transfer to a partner is not the same thing as a Bible distributed.

BWR plans to distinguish between Bibles handed to a partner and Bibles actually given to recipients.

Transferred is a custody event. Distributed is an impact event.

Approved distribution partners will eventually use the planned online Partner Center to submit simple reports showing the number of Bibles received, the number distributed during the reporting period, and the number still remaining.

Reports may also capture a general date or period, service area, distribution setting, and optional operational notes. They should not require names or identifying information about individual recipients.

This gives BWR a cleaner inventory trail and produces stronger public reporting: not merely how many Bibles left our possession, but how many were actually placed with people.

Campaign FAQ

What we can say clearly today.

When is the campaign planned to begin?

The current plan is to launch publicly in late May 2027 and continue fundraising for approximately six months, through late November 2027.

What is the planned fundraising goal?

The planned first-campaign goal is $50,000.

Where does BWR plan to purchase Bibles?

The Prayer Project currently intends to use Mardel as the primary vendor. Mardel SKU 3660396 is the preferred Bible option presently identified for the initiative. Final pricing, availability, quantities, and purchasing terms remain subject to change before launch.

Does naming Mardel mean Mardel is an official sponsor or partner?

No. Naming Mardel describes the current purchasing plan. Unless separately announced, it should not be read as a claim of sponsorship, endorsement, donation, or formal program partnership.

How will partner-distributed Bibles be counted?

BWR plans to have approved distribution partners submit simple online reports through a future Partner Center, distinguishing Bibles received, Bibles actually distributed, and Bibles still remaining.

Can I contribute right now?

No. BWR is currently in pre-launch development. The approved support method will be published when the campaign officially opens.

Will recipients pay for Bibles?

No. Bibles purchased through BWR are intended to be provided free of charge.

The 2027 Bibles Within Reach Campaign

The goal is not to finish with $50,000. The goal is to finish with Scripture within reach.

The campaign total is a tool. The real measure comes later—in Bibles purchased carefully, inventory tracked honestly, partnerships built responsibly, and people receiving the Word of God without cost or pressure.
