Why This Goal

Enough room to think beyond a one-day Bible drive.

A $50,000 goal gives Bibles Within Reach room to pursue meaningful Bible quantities while also planning for the practical work required to move them from a supplier into responsible distribution.

Bible purchasing is the heart of the campaign. But getting Bibles into real hands may also require shipping, packaging, transport, temporary storage, outreach materials, or other reasonable initiative costs. Those details should be disclosed clearly instead of hidden inside a simple “dollars equals Bibles” promise.

Final supplier arrangements, approved Bible selections, pricing assumptions, and campaign procedures will be published before fundraising opens.

From Support to Scripture

A clear path from campaign resources to a Bible in someone's hands.

BWR is being designed around a straightforward process. The exact details will mature as partners and supplier arrangements are finalized, but the core path should remain easy to understand.

1
Listen

Understand real needs.

Direct outreach and trusted local relationships help identify where Bibles are actually wanted and where access is limited.

2
Purchase

Choose responsibly.

Price, readability, durability, supplier terms, quantities, shipping, and real distribution capacity are considered before orders are placed.

3
Place

Distribute in more than one way.

Some Bibles may be handed out directly through Prayer Project outreach; others may be placed through approved local partners who already serve people in need.

4
Report

Show what happened.

Campaign and distribution totals should tell supporters what resources accomplished, not simply that money was raised.

Why Partners Are Part of the Campaign Plan

Distribution should follow trust, not just geography.

The Prayer Project can serve directly, but we will never know every family, every neighborhood, every shelter, or every local need as well as people already doing the work. Approved partners can help BWR place Bibles more thoughtfully and extend the initiative beyond what one team could do alone.

That is why partnership development is happening before fundraising begins. We want distribution capacity to grow alongside the campaign—not become an afterthought after Bibles are purchased.

Campaign Standards

A bigger goal should come with stronger discipline.

The first BWR campaign should be ambitious without becoming careless. These principles are meant to keep the initiative grounded while it grows.

Purpose

Keep Bibles at the center.

The campaign exists to make Scripture more accessible. Operational decisions should serve that mission rather than compete with it.

Clarity

Say what we know—and what we do not.

Supplier, purchasing, and distribution details should be published when they are real, not guessed early for a cleaner headline.

Dignity

Never turn recipients into proof.

Impact should be demonstrated through responsible reporting without making a person perform gratitude or surrender privacy to receive a Bible.

Stewardship

Document the path.

Purchasing, inventory, transfers, partner distribution, and campaign totals should be tracked so the work can be reviewed and improved.

Campaign FAQ

What we can say clearly today.

When is the Bibles Within Reach campaign planned to begin?

The current plan is to launch publicly in late May 2027 and continue fundraising for approximately six months, through late November 2027.

What is the planned fundraising goal?

The planned first-campaign goal is $50,000. Final supplier, purchasing, campaign, and distribution details will be published before the public fundraiser opens.

Can I contribute right now?

No. Bibles Within Reach is currently in pre-launch development, and this website is not accepting contributions for the initiative yet. The approved support method will be published when the campaign officially opens.

What will campaign resources be used for?

Bible purchasing is the central purpose of the campaign. Reasonable initiative-related needs may also include shipping, packaging, storage, transportation, or other costs necessary to move Bibles from a supplier into responsible distribution. Final campaign details will be disclosed before launch.

Where will the first Bibles go?

Tulsa, Oklahoma is the initiative's first service area. The current plan combines direct Prayer Project outreach with appropriate placement through trusted local churches, ministries, shelters, or community organizations.

Will recipients pay for Bibles?

No. Bibles purchased through Bibles Within Reach are intended to be provided free of charge to the people receiving them.

Will anyone be pressured to accept a Bible?

No. Distribution should be voluntary and respectful. No one should be pressured to receive a Bible or required to participate in another activity as a condition of receiving one.

Can organizations become partners before the campaign launches?

Yes. Partnership conversations are beginning during the pre-launch stage because distribution planning, supplier relationships, local knowledge, and outreach capacity all benefit from being developed before fundraising begins.
